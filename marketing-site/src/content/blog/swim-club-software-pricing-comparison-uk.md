If your committee is discussing whether to invest in [swim club management software](/swim-club-management-software), the first question is almost always: "How much does it cost?" It is a fair question, but the answer is rarely straightforward. Pricing models vary wildly between providers, and the headline figure often hides additional costs for features, support, or integrations.

This guide breaks down the real cost of the main UK swim club management software options in 2026, so your Treasurer can present clear numbers at the next committee meeting or AGM.

## The short answer

UK swim club management software ranges from **free to around £650 per year**, depending on the provider and your club's size. Budget-conscious committees often start by comparing [Club Organiser](/compare/cluborganiser) against other options to understand the feature-to-cost trade-offs. Here is a quick summary before we dig into the details:

| Software         | Approximate Annual Cost                | Pricing Model        |

| Club Organiser   | ~£300/yr                               | Monthly subscription |

These are software-only costs. You will also want to factor in payment processing fees if you use integrated billing (typically 1-2% per transaction for Direct Debit providers like GoCardless).

## SwimClub Manager: £649 per year

[SwimClub Manager](/compare/swimclubmanager) is the most established name in UK swim club software. It has been around for over a decade and holds Swim England API approval.

**What you get for £649/yr:**

- Member database and [registration management](/features/membership)
- Session and squad management with [attendance tracking](/features/attendance)
- Swim England API integration (automatic membership syncing)
- Mobile app for parents and coaches
- Competition entry management
- Financial tracking and invoicing

**What to watch for:**

- The annual cost is fixed regardless of club size, which means smaller clubs (under 100 members) pay the same as clubs with 300+
- Some clubs report that the interface feels dated compared to newer alternatives
- Setup and data migration support varies

For a club with 150 members, that works out at roughly **£4.33 per member per year**. For a club with 50 members, it is **£12.98 per member per year**, which is significantly more expensive on a per-member basis.

## Club Organiser: ~£300 per year

[Club Organiser](/compare/cluborganiser) is a budget-friendly option at **£24.95 per month** (approximately £300 per year). It is aimed at smaller to mid-sized clubs and offers a simpler feature set.

**What you get for ~£300/yr:**

- Member database
- Session management
- Hy-Tek and SportSystems file compatibility
- Basic financial tracking
- Communication tools

**What to watch for:**

- Monthly subscription gives flexibility (no annual lock-in)
- Feature set is more limited than SwimClub Manager or Swimly
- Check whether Swim England API integration is included or planned

For a 150-member club, that is roughly **£2 per member per year**, making it the most affordable paid option.

## Swimly: Free to £348 per year

[Swimly](/pricing) takes a different approach to pricing. Clubs with **fewer than 50 members pay nothing**. For clubs with 50 or more members, it is a flat **£29 per month** (£348 per year).

**What you get:**

- Full [member database](/features/membership) with [parent portal](/features/parent-portal)
- Session management with poolside [attendance tracking](/features/attendance) (mobile-optimised)
- Automated [billing](/features/billing) with Direct Debit integration
- Squad management and coach tools
- [Compliance tracking](/features/compliance) (Wavepower, DBS)
- Competition entry management
- Built-in communication tools

**What makes the pricing different:**

- The free tier means very small clubs or new clubs can use proper software without any budget commitment
- Flat-rate pricing means no surprises as your club grows (a club with 50 members and a club with 300 members pay the same)
- No annual contract, cancel any time
- **46% cheaper than SwimClub Manager** at the paid tier

For a 150-member club, that is roughly **£2.32 per member per year**. For a club of 50, it works out at **£6.96 per member per year** (or free if you are under 50 members).

**What to watch for:**

- Built for the US market, adapted for UK clubs

## Swim Manager: contact for pricing

**What you get:**

**What to watch for:**

## The "free" option: spreadsheets

Many clubs still manage everything in spreadsheets. While there is no software cost, the hidden expenses are significant:

- **Volunteer time**: A Membership Secretary spending 5 hours per week on spreadsheet administration is giving up 260 hours per year. At even a modest value of £12/hr, that is over **£3,000 in volunteer time**
- **Error risk**: Manual data entry leads to mistakes in membership records, payment tracking, and compliance documentation
- **Knowledge loss**: When the spreadsheet expert leaves the committee (which happens at every AGM), the new person inherits a system only one person understood
- **Compliance gaps**: GDPR, Wavepower, and DBS tracking in spreadsheets is fragile and difficult to audit

The real cost of spreadsheets is not zero. It is hidden in volunteer burnout, payment chasing, and compliance anxiety.

## What to actually budget

When presenting software costs to your committee, frame it properly:

**Annual software cost** is the headline number, but compare it against:

- Hours your Membership Secretary currently spends on admin (track this for a month)
- Time your Treasurer spends chasing failed standing orders or cash payments
- Risk cost of a Wavepower compliance gap or GDPR breach
- The cost of losing a good volunteer because the admin burden is too high

A useful benchmark: **if the software saves your committee 5 hours per month** in admin time, and you value volunteer time at £12/hour, that is **£720 per year in recovered time**. Every option on this list costs less than that.

## Payment processing: the hidden cost

Most modern swim club software integrates with payment processors for membership fees. The typical costs:

- **GoCardless (Direct Debit)**: 1-2% per transaction, no monthly fee
- **Stripe (card payments)**: 1.5% + 20p per transaction
- **Standing orders**: Free, but manual reconciliation and chasing failures

If your club collects £30,000 per year in membership fees via Direct Debit, expect to pay roughly **£300-600 in processing fees**. That is significantly less than the volunteer time spent chasing bounced standing orders or cash payments.

## How to decide

Here is a practical framework for your committee:

1. **Count your members**. If you are under 50, Swimly's free tier means you can try proper software with zero risk
2. **Calculate your current admin hours**. If your Membership Secretary or Treasurer spends more than 3 hours per week on admin, software will pay for itself
3. **Check Swim England API support**. If your club needs automated membership syncing with Swim England, confirm the software supports it
4. **Request demos**. Every provider offers demonstrations. See the software with your own club's data before committing
5. **Consider the committee handover test**. Could a new Treasurer or Membership Secretary pick this up at the next AGM without extensive training?
