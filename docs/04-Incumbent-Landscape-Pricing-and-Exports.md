### Importer acceptance checklist

1. Identify vendor version, country, club permissions, export date and any applied filters.
2. Request redacted examples preserving headers and relationships, including two children sharing a guardian and a child with multiple guardians.
3. Map source IDs, names, dates of birth, guardian contacts, classes, membership number/expiry and billing status explicitly. Membership validity and staff safeguarding records are separate data sets.
4. Preserve consent dates, versions and evidence only where supplied. Never infer consent from a contact or booking row.
5. Preview missing/ambiguous relationships and duplicate rows before applying an import; retain the source-to-target mapping for repeat runs.
6. Verify payment-account ownership and mandate portability separately from person-record import. Do not convert pending or requested collections into paid invoices.

Retain the generic mapper and existing implemented importers. Prioritise further vendor-specific work from real club samples, rather than assuming the public help pages define a stable schema.
