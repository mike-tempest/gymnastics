# UK gymnastics club software: competitive evidence

**Checked:** 15 September 2026. **Issue:** TEM-7. **Audience:** product, TEM-25 marketing and TEM-26 founding-club programme.

This refresh supersedes the 5 September comparison. That research remains in Git history before this revision; its prices, market-size estimates, review scores and negative feature claims must not be reused as current evidence. This pass covers JustGo, ClassForKids and Thrive4 as UK alternatives, with iClassPro and Jackrabbit as workflow benchmarks. It does not establish market share or test usability.

## Findings that change our positioning

1. **Automatic offers are not exclusive to Tumblebase.** iClassPro documents priority ordering, expiring offers and fallthrough. Compete on the complete club workflow and its demonstrated reliability, not an unsupported claim to be the only provider.
2. **Thrive4 has meaningful gymnastics compliance functionality.** Its insurance workflow matches imported membership reports, flags expiry and sends reminders. This is different from staff DBS, qualifications and safeguarding case management, which remain separate comparisons.
3. **Thrive4 is not limited to a proprietary payment rail.** Its current help centre documents linking existing GoCardless accounts as well as London & Zurich. The old payment-ownership comparison is withdrawn.
4. **JustGo is a substantial operations competitor.** Its BG offering includes recurring sessions, trials, attendance, renewals and direct BG integration. A free subscription does not mean free payment processing.
5. **ClassForKids has exports and flexible subscription plans.** Financial spreadsheets and register downloads are documented. Its help centre describes sibling and discounted plans, although discount codes do not apply to subscriptions. Neither “no exports” nor “no sibling discounts” is supportable.

The source register below supports these findings. “Not established” means this review lacks sufficient evidence; it does not mean a capability is absent.

### Limits that matter

- iClassPro's automated offers apply to eligible class waitlists, not camps or future-start classes. Acceptance proceeds through checkout. Availability, configuration and plan entitlement should be confirmed for a UK club. [I1]
- ClassForKids uses one subscription plan per parent/carer; staff can move families to sibling, multi-class or discounted plans. This is a manual-plan comparison, not absence of discounts. [C2]
- A membership-insurance alert does not prove a complete safeguarding system. Compare staff credentials, access control and case handling separately.
- Marketing pages demonstrate vendor claims. They do not establish ease of use, reliability, customer adoption or independent performance rankings.

## Product implications and marketing handoff

These are product recommendations inferred from the evidence, not vendor facts or claims that Tumblebase has completed every journey:

- **TEM-22 / TEM-55:** prove accepted place, completed details/consents, active payment method and confirmed first collection separately. The reopening of TEM-22 means “one-click active, billed gymnast” remains an acceptance target.
- **TEM-56 / TEM-57 / TEM-58:** trials, recurring terms and transfers are competitive requirements, not automatically differentiators.
- **TEM-61:** benchmark membership report matching and actionable expiry against Thrive4.
- **TEM-63:** benchmark parent make-ups against Jackrabbit and iClassPro, while keeping the current product scope.
- **TEM-25 / TEM-26:** use “Club operations, Direct Debit billing and safeguarding workflows, designed for gymnastics clubs.” Describe working alongside My BG without implying a native integration or BG endorsement.

Evidence-supported comparisons may say that JustGo's BG offering has no subscription charge but charges for online transactions, and that ClassForKids documents recurring card subscriptions. Do not say that nobody else automates waiting lists, that Thrive4 forces a proprietary rail, that ClassForKids has no exports, or that competitors lack all compliance tools. Do not publish a savings guarantee without a club-specific, VAT-consistent calculation.

The handoff wording is a research recommendation for review under TEM-25/26, not evidence that those issues or publication approval are complete.
